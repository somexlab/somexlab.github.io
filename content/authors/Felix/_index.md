I am a PhD student at the Chinese University of Hong Kong under the supervision of [Prof. To Ngai](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/research/NgaiToGroup/index.html) since Aug 2024. My research focuses on the in situ study of interfacial dynamics of particles at the nanoscale utilizing differential dynamic microscopy.

In September 2026, I started my research visit at SoMeX lab as an ÖAD scholar under the [Ernst Mach Grant – Worldwide](https://oead.at/en/study-research-teaching/overview-grants-and-scholarships/ernst-mach-grant) scholarship programme to gain deeper experience in differential dynamic microscopy and soft matter, specifically in the study of dynamics of soft microgels at fluid‒fluid interfaces.
