<div style="text-align: justify;">

I am an experimental soft matter physicist specialised in scattering techniques for the investigation of structural and dynamical properties of soft materials. Since October 2026, I have joined the SoMeX Lab as a postdoc, working with [Roberto]({{< relref "/authors/admin/_index.md" >}}) on the microgravity project [NEFCoEG](({{< relref "/project/26-04-01-NEFCoEG/index.md" >}})) (ESA project on Sedimenting Colloids) funded by FFG.

During my PhD at the Soft Matter Lab in [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it/en/), supervised by [Roberto Piazza](https://www.cmic.polimi.it/persone/docenti-e-ricercatori/piazza-roberto/), I investigated colloidal crystallization induced by thermal gradients using optical microscopy, and microscopic dynamics and microrheology of biologically relevant gels with advanced light scattering methods. 

As a postdoc at [Laboratoire Charles Coulomb](https://coulomb.umontpellier.fr/) (CNRS, University of Montpellier), with [Laurence Ramos](https://coulomb.umontpellier.fr/en/laurence-ramos-winner-of-the-maurice-couette-grand-prize/) and [Luca Cipelletti](https://www.essc.esf.org/luca-cipelletti/), I designed an experimental platform combining spatially-resolved dynamic light scattering with fluorescence imaging to investigate the enzymatic degradation of biopolymer solutions and gels. During this period, I have also worked in synchrotron X-ray facilities and developed a general interest on colloids diffusion in crowded environments, soft glasses and X-ray induced synthesis of nanoparticles.

In the framework of the NEFCoEG project in the SoMeX lab, I am studying equilibrium and non-equilibrium fluctuations in settling colloidal suspensions under effective gravity fields.
