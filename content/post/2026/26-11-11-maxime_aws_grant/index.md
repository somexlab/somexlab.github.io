We are delighted to announce that our postdoctoral researcher, [Maxime]({{< relref "/authors/Maxime/_index.md" >}}), has been awarded the **Austria Wirtschaftsservice Gesellschaft mbH (aws) Proof of Concept Grant**, amounting to approximately €80.000.

The grant will support the development of an innovative technique for measuring charge in colloidal systems, with the potential to pave the way for future technological applications and entrepreneurship.

[Austria Wirtschaftsservice (aws)](https://www.aws.at/en/) supports innovative ideas and helps researchers and aspiring entrepreneurs translate promising concepts into market-ready solutions and establish their own startups.

***Congratulations, Maxime, on this fantastic achievement!*** 🎉
