Continuing the tradition of once-a-year group activities outside the workplace, this year the Somex group enjoyed a fun trip to [Römerstadt Carnuntum](https://www.carnuntum.at/en), the remarkable ancient Roman city on the outskirts of Vienna. The day was filled with fascinating history, guided exploration, and plenty of opportunities to relax, connect, and share a few laughs. It was a great chance to step away from our daily routines, strengthen team spirit, and create memorable experiences together.
