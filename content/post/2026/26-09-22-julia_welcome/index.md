We are glad to welcome [Julia Jezovic]({{< relref "/authors/Julia/_index.md" >}}) to our research group at the University of Vienna! 

After exploring the fascinating worlds of particle physics and isotope physics, Julia is now embarking on a new scientific journey into the world of biophysics. Building on the research of our PhD student [Jasmin]({{< relref "/authors/Julia/_index.md" >}}), she will further investigate the intriguing phenomenon of **cellular jamming**, contributing fresh perspectives and insights to our growing understanding of the field.

We are excited to see where Julia’s curiosity and expertise will take her and look forward to the discoveries she will bring to our group.

***Welcome to the team, Julia! 🎉***
