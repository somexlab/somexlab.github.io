We are delighted to welcome [Vincenzo Ruzzi]({{< relref "/authors/Vincenzo/_index.md" >}}) to our research group at the University of Vienna! 

Vincenzo joins us as an experienced postdoctoral researcher, having previously worked at the [Laboratoire Charles Coulomb](https://coulomb.umontpellier.fr/). His expertise lies in optics and light scattering, and he will be working with us to investigate colloidal sedimentation under microgravity conditions.

We are excited to have Vincenzo on board and look forward to the new insights and knowledge his work will bring to the field.

***Welcome to the team, Vincenzo! 🎉***
