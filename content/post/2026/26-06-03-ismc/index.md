It was a fruitful and enjoyable week for the Somex group at the ISMC 2026 conference held in [BITS Pilani](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/goa/), Goa, India. For many members, this was their first visit to India, making the experience both scientifically rewarding and culturally memorable.

The [conference](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/ismc2026) brought together numerous leading researchers from the Soft Matter community and was among the largest soft matter meetings ever held in India. The scientific programme featured a wide range of exciting talks and discussions, providing excellent opportunities for exchanging ideas, establishing new collaborations, and showcasing our group's research.
