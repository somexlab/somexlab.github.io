We are delighted to welcome [Sophie Hermann]({{< relref "/authors/Sophie/_index.md" >}}) to our research group at the University of Vienna! 

As a theoretical physicist, Sophie brings a new perspective to our soft matter research, drawing on her strong background in statistical mechanics.

Sophie joins us as the recipient of the prestigious **[E-STEEM Fellowship 2026](https://careers.univie.ac.at/en/postdoc/e-steem)** awarded to female researchers in STEM and Economics. She previously held a [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships), further highlighting her outstanding research experience.

We are excited to see the new ideas and contributions Sophie will bring to our group and look forward to the research ahead.

***Welcome to the group, Sophie!***
