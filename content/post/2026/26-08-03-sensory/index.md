An unique event that aims to translate the knowledge of Soft Matter to *Sensory Science* and engineering. The [summer school](https://softsensory.sciencesconf.org/resource/page/id/7) was organized by [Institut d'Études Scientifiques de Cargèse](https://iesc.universita.corsica/?lang=en)(University of Corsica, France). This year's theme was: **Soft Matter and Sensory Perception**.


[Roberto]({{< relref "/authors/admin/_index.md" >}}) and our postdoctoral researcher, [Maxime]({{< relref "/authors/Maxime/_index.md" >}}), participated in the event. Maxime delivered a lecture on the use of Differential Dynamic Microscopy (DDM) for particle sizing and microrheology.


The programme featured a diverse range of inspiring lectures, interactive discussions, and a distinctive hands-on component. Participants carried out tabletop experiments alongside data analysis sessions, including fractional modelling, providing a unique opportunity to connect theory with practical applications.
