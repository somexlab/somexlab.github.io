We are delighted to announce that **SoMeX is among the first research groups at the University of Vienna to achieve the Bronze SPARKHub Certificate!** 🎉

[SPARKHub](https://sparkhub.eu/), developed by UKRI in collaboration with research communities, supports environmentally sustainable academic research by providing practical guidance, good-practice resources, and certification to help research groups reduce their environmental impact.

This achievement reflects our commitment to making research more sustainable and to continuously improving our practices.

**Congratulations to the entire SoMeX team! 🥉🌱**
