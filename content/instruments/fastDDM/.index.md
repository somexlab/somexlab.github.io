### Why use FastDDM

<div style="text-align: justify;">

Differential Dynamics Microscopy (DDM) is a technique that enables us to measure particle dynamics across multiple length and time scales using a unique image analysis algorithm. Using this technique, a microscope can be converted into a Dynamic Light Scattering (DLS) . Bright-field DDM has been used successfully to study the behaviour of small proteins, colloids as well as bacteria.

</div>
